**Type:** experimental. **Reviewed:** 2026-09-13.

## Source

[Original / authoritative source](https://doi.org/10.1145/1753326.1753357) · [Full paper](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~jheer/files/2010-MTurk-CHI.pdf)

## Scope

## Limitations

Replicates and extends proportional judgment experiments online. Accuracy rankings are task- and stimulus-dependent; angle did not perform worse than length in this experiment. The guide does not use a universal ordering or claim the queue was tested.
