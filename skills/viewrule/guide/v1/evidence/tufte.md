**Type:** practitioner. **Reviewed:** 2026-09-13.

## Source

[Original / authoritative source](https://www.edwardtufte.com/book/the-visual-display-of-quantitative-information/)

## Scope

## Limitations

Practitioner design principles, not a controlled comparison of modern interactive interfaces. The publisher identifies the edition and topics; this guide paraphrases principles without reproducing book illustrations.
